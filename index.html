How it works

A process built for speed without cutting corners

Every search follows the same disciplined process, adapted to your role and team.

Scope the role

We start with a working session to understand the role, the team, and what success looks like in six months.

Source & screen

We go beyond inbound applicants to actively source and pre-vet candidates against your specific bar.

Shortlist & brief

You meet a focused shortlist, each with a written summary of fit, motivation, and risk areas.

Close the hire

We manage references, offer strategy, and negotiation so the process ends in an accepted offer.

About

Led by someone who's built talent functions from scratch — twice

Eryn McHugh

Founder, Footprint Talent Advisory

Eryn founded the original Footprint Talent in 2010 and spent eight years running executive searches for PE- and VC-backed companies — placing board members, CFOs, CHROs, and General Counsel where the wrong hire would have been very expensive.

She then spent nearly a decade on the other side of the table, building talent and people functions from the ground up inside fast-growing companies — running talent acquisition and, later, all of people operations as Chief People Officer. That operating experience is what shapes how Footprint Talent Advisory works today: less like a vendor filling a req, more like a partner who has actually owned the hiring plan, the budget, and the outcome.

She founded Footprint Talent Advisory to bring that same standard to PE-backed and growth-stage SaaS and professional services companies scaling their people and talent functions from the ground up.
